# Referral digest automation

Schedule this prompt at 2:00 PM and 7:00 PM in Asia/Kolkata on the primary machine.

At each scheduled run, prepare and send the candidate's LinkedIn referral digest for every qualifying job-alert email that has not already appeared in a successfully sent referral digest.

Use Asia/Kolkata for all date and time decisions. Before collecting jobs, inspect Sent mail in the signed-in Gmail account and identify the most recent successfully sent referral digest. Treat the latest covered job-alert timestamp or covered Job IDs in that digest as the checkpoint.

Collect every qualifying job alert received after that checkpoint, including alerts belonging to an earlier scheduled window or a previous calendar day. This is mandatory catch-up behavior. A missed 2:00 PM run must be absorbed by the 7:00 PM run, and missed runs across an offline period must be absorbed by the first run after the machine becomes available. Never resend a Job ID already covered by an earlier digest.

Use the signed-in Gmail and LinkedIn sessions when available. Include all distinct unprocessed openings. For each opening:

1. Open and verify the job listing.
2. Record the exact company, job title, standalone Job ID or requisition ID, location, and working job link.
3. Match the job description only against verified facts in the candidate's local resume.
4. Search LinkedIn for the best referral contact in this order: recruiter visibly hiring for that role, exact hiring manager, relevant engineering manager, relevant talent-acquisition partner, adjacent team member, then the most relevant verified employee.
5. Search a second time when the first result is weak. Contacts may be anywhere in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, NCR, or Mumbai.
6. Include the contact's name, current role, clickable LinkedIn profile, and a concise explanation of why the person was chosen.
7. Write a natural, succinct, directly copy-pasteable LinkedIn message. Use proper paragraph breaks. Introduce the candidate and cite only specific resume experience relevant to that job. Put the Job ID on its own line. Put the referral request in a separate final paragraph with a clear CTA asking whether the person would be comfortable referring the candidate. Do not use em dashes. Do not use placeholders or invented skills.
8. If a listing or contact cannot be fully verified, label the issue clearly, select the strongest available contact after a second search, and explain what should be checked. Do not omit the job.
9. Prioritize the strongest opportunities at the top and identify hold items separately.

Consolidate everything into one readable HTML email table with a prominent "GENERATED BY CHATGPT" header and columns for company, job details, recommended contact, rationale, LinkedIn profile, and copy-paste message.

At the 2:00 PM run, include all unprocessed jobs since the last successful digest. At the 7:00 PM run, include all remaining unprocessed jobs. If a scheduled window was missed, include its backlog automatically. If there are no unprocessed jobs for a run, send a short HTML summary stating that no new referral opportunities were found.

Verify that the email appears in Sent before advancing the checkpoint. If sending fails, do not mark any jobs as covered; retry them at the next run.

Do not send LinkedIn connection requests or LinkedIn messages. Do not expose Gmail credentials, resume-derived data, recipient information, browser cookies, or referral-table contents in Git. Keep only generic reusable code and prompts in the repository.
